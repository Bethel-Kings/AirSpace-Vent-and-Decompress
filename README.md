# AirSpace 🌬️

### *Life gets heavy. Let it out.*

AirSpace is an **AI-powered emotional decompression platform** designed to help people safely release emotional stress, organise overwhelming thoughts, and regain mental clarity.

Unlike traditional journaling apps or AI companions, AirSpace focuses on **constructive emotional venting**. It provides a secure, judgment-free environment where users can freely express themselves through voice or text before being guided toward reflection and actionable next steps.

> **From emotional overload to mental clarity—in minutes.**

---

## 🌟 Why AirSpace?

Modern life is overwhelming.

Whether it's work pressure, academic stress, relationship conflicts, financial worries, or everyday frustrations, people often have nowhere safe to process their emotions.

Many end up:

* Bottling everything up.
* Oversharing with friends who may not always be available.
* Doomscrolling or venting on social media.
* Replaying negative thoughts without resolution.

AirSpace offers a healthier alternative.

---

## 🚀 Features

* 🎙️ **Voice & Text Venting**

  * Express yourself freely using voice or text.

* 🤖 **AI-Guided Reflection**

  * Transform emotional chaos into structured, actionable insights.

* 📊 **Mood Tracking**

  * Track emotional states before and after each session.

* 🔒 **Privacy First**

  * Secure by design with encrypted data and user-controlled privacy.

* 🧠 **Emotion Recognition**

  * Identify recurring emotions, stressors, and behavioural patterns over time.

* 📈 **Progress Dashboard**

  * Visualise your emotional well-being and personal growth.
